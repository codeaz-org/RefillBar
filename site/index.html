See your Claude, Amp and Grok usage in the menu bar

UsageBar is a small macOS app that shows how much of each plan you have used and when it resets. No API keys: you sign in to each site once, like in a browser.

Install

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return. It downloads the app into Applications and opens it.

curl -fsSL https://usagebar.codeaz.org/install.sh | bash

Needs macOS 14 or later. Built for both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. You can read the script first: it is 20 lines.

Why a Terminal line and not a download button?

The app is free and not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS blocks it when it is downloaded through a browser. Installing from Terminal avoids that block. If you prefer, download the zip and then allow it under System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. Or build it from source, which is one file of Swift.

What it does

  • Shows one small meter per usage limit in the menu bar. Meters turn orange at 70% used and red at 90%.
  • Lists each limit in the menu with percent used, time left until reset, and the reset time.
  • Reloads the pages on a timer you choose, from 15 seconds to 5 minutes.
  • Keeps your logins on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the three sites themselves.

Good to know

  • It reads the usage pages of claude.ai, ampcode.com and grok.com. When one of them changes its page, readings can break until the app is updated.
  • It reloads those pages in your signed-in session. Check that this is fine under each provider's terms.
  • It is a personal tool shared as-is, with no support promised.
